## 正文

InfoQ has opened registration for six [online cohort workshops](https://certification.qconferences.com/?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) starting between January 9 and 22, 2027. Covering AI Engineering, Software Architecture, and High-Performing Teams, the five-week programs bring senior engineers, architects, and engineering leaders together to work through technical and organizational decisions with experienced practitioners.

The workshops go beyond studying technical material. Participants apply frameworks from [QCon and InfoQ](https://qconferences.com/?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) to decisions from their own work, compare approaches with practitioners from other organizations, and return to discuss what they tried and learned. Working across different organizational contexts helps expose trade-offs and assumptions that may not emerge from an internal discussion alone.

Each cohort combines four hours of live facilitated sessions per week, independent application work, and a program-specific capstone.

## AI Engineering: Retrieval, Agents, and Evaluation

Production AI involves decisions beyond model selection: how context is retrieved and maintained, how much autonomy agents should have, and how reliability, latency, and inference costs are managed.

The [InfoQ AI Engineering Program](https://certification.qconferences.com/ai-engineering?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) examines these decisions through RAG architectures, agent design, platforms, and evaluation. Participants work through technical approaches and failure modes, applying them to AI systems or proposals from their own work.

Three January cohorts are planned:

-   [Hien Luu](https://certification.qconferences.com/ai/ai-engineering/jan-2027-americas-hien-luu?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27), AI/ML leader and author of *MLOps with Ray*, starts January 9, Saturdays at 09:00 PST.
-   [Adi Polak](https://certification.qconferences.com/ai/ai-engineering/jan-2027-americas-adi-polak?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27), Director of Developer Experience Engineering at Confluent, starts January 21, Thursdays at 08:00 PST.
-   [Lan Chu](https://certification.qconferences.com/ai/ai-engineering/jan-2027-europe-lan-chu?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27), principal engineer at Rabobank and author of *Post-training LLMs*, starts January 22, Fridays at 14:00 CET. Her QCon London 2026 [presentation](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/production-rag-retrieval-architecture-evals/?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) examined a production RAG system built on more than 10,000 documents and used by more than 300 people.

## Architecture: Decisions Across Teams and Platforms

Architecture choices are shaped by system requirements, team boundaries, platform ownership, and organizational constraints.

The [InfoQ Architect Program](https://certification.qconferences.com/architecture?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) covers distributed systems, decentralized decisions, platform engineering, and AI architecture. Participants apply the material to architecture decisions they currently own, compare trade-offs with peers, and practice communicating their reasoning to stakeholders.

The capstone involves co-authoring a technical article examining AI's role in software architecture or platform engineering. Selected articles may be published on InfoQ.

Both cohorts start January 22 and are facilitated by Luca Mezzalira, independent software architect and author of *Building Micro-Frontends*. Friday sessions are offered at [09:30](https://certification.qconferences.com/architecture/architect/jan-2027-europe-luca-mezzalira?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) GMT and [10:00](https://certification.qconferences.com/architecture/architect/jan-2027-americas-luca-mezzalira?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) EST.

## High-Performing Teams: Delivery Flow, Ownership, and AI

Faster coding does not necessarily improve delivery when reviews, approvals, or infrastructure dependencies become the constraint. AI agents also change how responsibilities are divided and which work requires human judgment.

The [InfoQ High-Performing Teams Program](https://certification.qconferences.com/high-performing-teams?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) examines team structure, ownership, delivery flow, AI responsibilities, and engineering metrics as one operating model.

Participants evaluate constraints in their own team environments, compare approaches with peers, and develop a proposed target operating model covering responsibilities, workflow, and measurement.

The [January 12 cohort](https://certification.qconferences.com/leadership/high-performing-teams/jan-2027-europe-olimpiu-pop?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27) is facilitated by Olimpiu Pop, InfoQ Podcast co-host with experience in solutions architecture, engineering management, and CTO roles. Sessions run on Tuesdays and Thursdays, from 16:00 to 18:00 CET.

## Workshop Format and Participation

The live online workshops focus on discussion and application rather than recorded lectures. Participants work with an experienced facilitator and a small cross-company peer group, bringing current engineering decisions into the sessions and testing approaches between meetings.

Sessions are not recorded, allowing participants to discuss organizational constraints more openly. Certification requires attendance, active participation, and successful completion of the program capstone.

Participation costs USD 1,470 per person. Full schedules, syllabi, and registration details are available on the [InfoQ Online Certification Programs website](https://certification.qconferences.com/?utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=newspost_nov17_onlinecohortsjan27).
