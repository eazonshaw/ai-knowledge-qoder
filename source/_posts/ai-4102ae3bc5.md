This week's Java roundup for October 5th, 2026, features news highlighting: OpenJDK JEPs Proposed to Target for JDK 28; Lazy Constants elevated to Candidate status; the GA releases of Apache Grails 8.0 and Open Liberty 20.0.0.10; point releases of TornadoVM, Infinispan, LangChain4j and Spock; and a maintenance release of GlassFish.

#### OpenJDK

JEP 546, [Adaptive Heap Sizing for ZGC](https://openjdk.org/jeps/546), has been [elevated](https://mail.openjdk.org/archives/list/jdk-dev@openjdk.org/thread/WV32SL2H5BF3QCJG354G75ANEJAK5N3Z/) from **Candidate** to **Proposed to Target** for JDK 28. This JEP proposes to "*enhance the Z Garbage Collector to adaptively adjust the size of the heap based on the resources available, the application's needs, and the needs of neighboring applications contending for the same resources*." The review is expected to conclude on Wednesday, October 14, 2026.

JEP 545, [Faster Startup and Warmup with ZGC](https://openjdk.org/jeps/545), has been [elevated](https://mail.openjdk.org/archives/list/jdk-dev@openjdk.org/thread/CIZZHR336325FT3GES4RUBMPSIEQMFEW/) from **Candidate** to **Proposed to Target** for JDK 28. This JEP proposes to "*improve application startup and warmup by enhancing the Z Garbage Collector to acquire and prepare physical memory more swiftly and efficiently in response to application needs*." The review is expected to conclude on Wednesday, October 14, 2026.

JEP 543, [Structured Concurrency](https://openjdk.org/jeps/543), has been [elevated](https://mail.openjdk.org/archives/list/jdk-dev@openjdk.org/thread/CXGNFVRWSQB4D62JHSQGCTJ3YXU6AMHN/) from **Candidate** to **Proposed to Target** for JDK 28. This JEP proposes to finalize this feature, without change, after seven rounds of preview, delivered in JDK 21 through the upcoming release of JDK 27, and two rounds of incubator delivered in JDK 19 and JDK 20. This feature simplifies concurrent programming by introducing structured concurrency to "*treat groups of related tasks running in different threads as a single unit of work, thereby streamlining error handling and cancellation, improving reliability, and enhancing observability.*" The review is expected to conclude on Tuesday, October 13, 2026.

JEP 547, [Lazy Constants (Fourth Preview)](https://openjdk.org/jeps/547), has been [elevated](https://mail.openjdk.org/archives/list/jdk-dev@openjdk.org/thread/NHHDKKPJQG3ZXKOIYU2LDR5NHBSEISMQ/) from its **JEP Draft 8389764** to **Candidate** status. This JEP proposes a fourth preview, with no changes, after three rounds of preview delivered in the upcoming release of JDK 27 and JDK 26 through JDK 25. Formerly known as *Stable Values* and *Computed Constants*, this feature introduces the concept of *computed constants*, defined as immutable value holders that are initialized at most once. This offers the performance and safety benefits of **`final`** fields, while offering greater flexibility as to the timing of initialization. The name change from *Stable Values* to *Lazy Constants* with the release of JDK 26, better captures the intent of a high-level use case.
